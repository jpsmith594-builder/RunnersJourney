## Full description
Your running life is more than the kilometres you've covered. It's the races you've completed, the places you've visited, the medals you've earned and the memories you've made.

My Running Journal is the life diary for runners. Every race you run becomes a pin on your own map, so you can see where running has taken you and relive it any time.

PIN YOUR RACES ON A MAP
Add a race and it lands on the map in the town where you ran it. Zoom out to see your whole journey, zoom in to see a single finish line. Add upcoming races too, so your next adventure is already waiting on the map.

KEEP THE STORY, NOT JUST THE STATS
Save your finish time, distance, photos, medals and a few words about how the day felt. This is not a training log. It is a keepsake for the races that mattered.

GROW YOUR RUNNER CARD
Every finished race, new town, new country and medal builds your rating, from Novice up to Elite. Earn badges, celebrate personal bests, and share your runner card with friends and your club.

SEE YOUR YEAR
Switch on the optional journey trail to watch a faint dotted line flow from race to race. Filter by year, follow the numbered pins, and see where your story began and where it is now. Turn on vivid map colours for a richer map, or switch them off.

SET GOALS
Set a goal for the year and get a little celebration when you reach it.

RUN WITH YOUR CLUB
Pick your club from a shared list so everyone from the same club ends up in the same place.

MADE FOR RACE DAY
- Kilometres or miles, your choice
- Light and dark themes
- Works with your own races, anywhere in the world
- No ads

FREE TO START
Add up to 5 finished races with a photo on each, free. Upgrade for unlimited races and up to 10 photos per race. Prices are shown in your own currency in the store and may change.
