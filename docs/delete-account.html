My Running Journal

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and sign in.
  2. Open the menu, tap Profile, then Edit profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Cannot get into the app?

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you registered with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in record (email and password).
  • Your profile and profile photo.
  • All your races, race photos and notes.
  • Your race bucket list, friends connections and reactions.

What may be kept

If you subscribed, Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records as the law requires. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions first, so you are not charged again.
