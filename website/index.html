Questions

Good to know

Is this a training app?

No. There are great apps for logging every run. My Running Journal is for the races: the ones with a start line, a finish line and a story.

Who can see my races, photos and medals?

Only you, plus friends you accept. Club mates, if you opt in, see only your name. You can hide your medal wall from friends with one switch on the wall.

What happens if I stop subscribing?

You keep every race. If you have more than 5, they become read-only until you subscribe again or delete down to 5.

What do the pin colours mean?

They show the race distance: orange for 5K, green for 10K, teal for 15K, purple for a half marathon, burgundy for a marathon and blue for ultras. Tap a colour in the map legend to see only those races.

Kilometres or miles?

Your choice, any time in Settings. Races are stored in kilometres, so you can switch back and forth without losing anything.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes, inside the app or by request. See how to delete your account.

Is it on iPhone?

Android first. An iPhone version is planned once the Android app is out.
